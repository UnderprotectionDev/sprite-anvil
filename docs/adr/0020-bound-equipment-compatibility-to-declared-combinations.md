# Bound equipment compatibility to declared combinations

For feature candidate F3, Equipment Compatibility Scope consists of user-selected character, equipment, direction, and action combinations rather than every possible combination; untested, excluded, and incompatible combinations retain different meanings. Both separate components and complete results with baked-in equipment can carry evidence for their declared use, but evidence about a complete result establishes no interchangeable component compatibility and creates no inferred layers or history. The original 2026-09-21 opportunity-interview file is unavailable, so it is not cited as recoverable evidence; this decision controls the testing burden and preserves existing art at the cost of explicit coverage and evidence boundaries, and does not add F3 to the PRD's required scope.
