# Scope canonical designs by visual world and use context

A Subject Identity may own distinct Canonical Designs for different Visual Worlds and use contexts, and every derivative must bind to one exact canonical. One Asset Family is limited to one Visual World, use context, and Canonical Design lineage; representations that require different Canonical Designs belong to separate Asset Families linked by Subject Identity. Thus a gameplay sprite, illustrated portrait, UI representation, and marketing image can share identity without being forced into one family, resolution, or rendering contract. This preserves cross-world identity and prevents unrelated revalidation, at the cost of an additional identity layer, more families, and explicit canonical selection for derivatives.
