# Use dependency-aware erasure with tombstones

Permanent erasure will first show every dependent version, Export Bundle, and managed Project Archive. The user may erase all managed copies together or remove content while retaining an Erasure Tombstone that records only identity and broken-lineage facts; downloaded external copies cannot be recalled. Erasure runs as a trackable Deletion Job: active-store removal and backup purge are reported against a published retention deadline, and the user receives a completion receipt rather than an unverifiable claim of instantaneous deletion. This preserves the ability to remove sensitive or unlicensed material instead of making immutability absolute, at the cost of backup-aware deletion operations, deliberately invalidated historical reproducibility, and potentially deleting otherwise immutable managed delivery records.
