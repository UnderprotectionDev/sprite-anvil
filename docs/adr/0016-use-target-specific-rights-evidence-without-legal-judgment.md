# Use target-specific rights evidence without legal judgment

Rights Records are immutable, user-declared evidence with Documented, Assertion Only, Unknown, or Restricted states, and Rights Lineage keeps a result's own record distinct from the records of its Canonical Design, references, and dependent sources. A Delivery Target's Rights Evidence Policy decides which records and states are acceptable for that target; the product evaluates the declared evidence but never determines legal validity or imposes a universal export ban. Later risk creates a Historical Risk Notice without rewriting an earlier Delivery Fulfilment. This makes commercial and prototype thresholds explicit while preserving historical truth, at the cost of more provenance work and leaving legal responsibility with the user.
